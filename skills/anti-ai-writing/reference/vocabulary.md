# Common AI Vocabulary, Phrases & Human Alternatives

A practical catalogue of words and phrases that appear at elevated frequency in LLM output, with simpler human substitutes and before/after rewrites. Use this file when editing for vocabulary, transitions, buzzwords, or filler.

No single word proves AI authorship. Signals matter when they **stack** — elevated vocabulary + formulaic transitions + low specificity in the same passage.

---

## Frequency observations (sourced)

Kobak et al. (2025), analyzing 15+ million PubMed abstracts, documented abrupt post-ChatGPT spikes in style words. Reported frequency ratios vs. pre-LLM baselines include:

| Word | Approx. excess ratio |
|------|----------------------|
| delve / delves | ~28× |
| underscores | ~13.8× |
| showcasing | ~10.7× |

Other words with elevated post-2022 usage in scientific prose include *pivotal*, *intricate*, *meticulously*, *realm*, *aligns*, *underpins*, *notably*, *comprehensive*, *insights*. Bloomberry's AI Sentence DNA corpus (2026) catalogues 7,400+ signal entries and treats co-occurrence — not isolated tokens — as the diagnostic unit.

Editorial note: word lists age as models update. Prefer structural and register patterns over any fixed ban-list.

## Buzzwords & corporate jargon

Avoid packing these together:

- move the needle
- game changer / game-changing
- paradigm shift
- next level
- best-in-class
- value-add / value proposition
- circle back
- low-hanging fruit
- thought leadership
- dig deeper
- deep dive
- North Star
- boil the ocean
- band-aid solution

**Rewrite rule:** replace intensity with a concrete claim.

- AI: "Our innovative platform will revolutionize business processes."
- Human: "Our new platform will cut invoice processing from two days to two hours."

## Common AI transition words

| AI phrase | AI example | Human alternative | Why it feels fake |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| That being said | "That being said, X should be used with caution." | However… / Even so… | Wordy formal contrast |
| At its core | "At its core, ML is pattern recognition." | Essentially… / (state claim) | Stilted summary frame |
| To put it simply | "To put it simply, deep learning mimics the brain." | Simply… / In plain terms… | Unnecessary emphasis |
| A key takeaway is | "A key takeaway is that AI complements humans." | The main point is… / (state it) | Canned summary cue |
| Furthermore / Moreover / Additionally | "Moreover, the system scales." | Also… / And… / (new sentence) | Over-signposted logic |
| In conclusion / Ultimately | "In conclusion, AI should complement humans." | (end without cue) | Robotic closer |
| In other words | "In other words, the system failed." | (omit restatement) | Explicit rephrase habit |
| At the end of the day | "At the end of the day, efficiency is key." | (omit) | Cliché filler |
| When it comes to | "When it comes to security, plan early." | For security… / On security… | High-frequency filler |
| On the other hand | "On the other hand, costs rise." | But… / Still… | Fine occasionally; stacked = AI |
| From a broader perspective | "From a broader perspective, markets shift." | More broadly… / (omit) | Essay scaffolding |
| Let's dive in | "Let's dive into the details." | Here's how it works. / (start) | Content-creator stock |
| Here's the thing | "Here's the thing: timing matters." | Timing matters. | Fake intimacy |
| In today's… | "In today's fast-paced world…" | (specific context) | Temporal landscape opener |

Bloomberry flags *at its core*, *when it comes to*, and *needless to say* as elevated across models; *let's unpack* is especially associated with ChatGPT-style hooks.

## Common AI phrases by category

### Productivity

- Take your productivity to the next level
- Work smarter, not harder
- Unlock your full potential
- Streamline your workflow
- Maximize your output
- Game-changing habits

**Prefer:** name the habit and the measurable effect.

### Business

- Drive business outcomes
- Align stakeholders
- Create synergies
- Deliver value at scale
- Navigate the competitive landscape
- Leverage best practices

**Prefer:** who does what, by when, with what result.

### Marketing

- Elevate your brand
- Captivate your audience
- Stand out in a crowded market
- Craft compelling narratives
- Fuel growth
- Convert like never before

**Prefer:** channel, offer, and proof.

### Technology

- Cutting-edge solution
- Seamlessly integrate
- Harness the power of AI
- Future-proof your stack
- End-to-end platform
- Next-generation architecture

**Prefer:** what it connects to, what it replaces, what breaks.

### Education

- Foster a love of learning
- Empower students to succeed
- Holistic approach to education
- Cultivate critical thinking
- In today's digital classroom

**Prefer:** the assignment, the skill, the evidence of learning.

### Motivation

- Believe in yourself
- The only limit is you
- Success leaves clues
- Embrace the journey
- Your future self will thank you

**Prefer:** a concrete decision or tradeoff.

### Newsletters

- Hope you're having a great week
- Quick one today
- Without further ado
- As always, thanks for reading
- Hit reply and tell me…
- Here's what's inside

**Prefer:** open with the news; keep ritual lines sparse.

### Tutorials

- In this tutorial, you will learn…
- By the end of this guide…
- Let's get started
- First things first
- And there you have it
- Congrats — you've successfully…

**Prefer:** start at step one; end when it works.

## Human alternatives: before / after

| Pattern | AI example | Human rewrite | Why AI feels off |
|---------|------------|---------------|------------------|
| Buzzword | "Our innovative platform will revolutionize business processes." | "Our new platform will greatly improve business processes." | Hype without detail |
| Transition | "That being said, we can implement X with caution." | "However, we can implement X with caution." | Wordy contrast cue |
| Stated lesson | "A key takeaway is that collaboration drives success." | "Collaboration drives success." | Announces the obvious |
| Contrast frame | "It's not about getting more done; it's about doing the right things." | "Focus on doing the right things, not just getting more done." | Classic antithesis template |
| Corporate jargon | "We leverage analytics to optimize customer engagement." | "We use analytics to improve customer engagement." | Leverage/optimize stack |
| Over-formal | "Please be advised that we have concluded the review." | "We have finished the review." | Legalistic tone |
| Hedged claim | "Arguably, this approach has the most robust framework." | "This approach has a strong framework." | Hedge + elevated vocab |
| Conclusion cue | "In conclusion, continuous learning is key." | "Continuous learning is key." | Signposted ending |
| Email filler | "It is my pleasure to inform you that…" | "I'm happy to tell you that…" | Stock courtesy |
| Delve | "Let's delve into the history of AI." | "Let's explore the history of AI." | Formal synonym for explore |
| Pivotal | "ML plays a pivotal role in automation." | "ML is central to automation." | Stock importance word |

## Editing checklist (vocabulary)

- [ ] Cut elevated synonyms when a plain word works
- [ ] Remove stacked transitions (*Moreover… Furthermore… Additionally…*)
- [ ] Delete throat-clearing (*It is worth noting…*)
- [ ] Replace buzzwords with specifics (numbers, names, mechanisms)
- [ ] Keep at most one "smart" word per passage — if any
- [ ] Read aloud; if it sounds like a pitch deck, rewrite

## 2025 to 2026 additions

Word lists age fast, so this section favours *kinds* of word over a ban list. Sources: Pangram, the tropes.fyi directory, the Wikipedia editors' guide, and the 2026 UWA write-up (see detection-and-models.md).

### Intensifier adverbs that add mood, not meaning

*quietly*, *deeply*, *genuinely*, *fundamentally*, *truly*, *remarkably*. Often pasted before a verb to add weight ("quietly transforming", "fundamentally changes"). Cut the adverb, or replace it with the fact that justifies the weight.

### Ornate nouns for simple things

*tapestry*, *landscape*, *paradigm*, *realm*, *ecosystem*, *journey*, *testament*. Say what the thing is: "the market", "the way we work", "the team".

### Pompous swaps for plain verbs

| AI phrasing | Plain |
|-------------|-------|
| serves as | is |
| stands as a testament to | shows |
| boasts | has |
| plays a crucial role in | matters for / affects |
| offers a range of | has / includes |
| facilitates | helps / allows |

### Hazy trailing significance (the "-ing" add-on)

Sentence ends with a participle clause that claims importance without saying what it is: *"...highlighting its importance"*, *"...reflecting broader trends"*, *"...underscoring the need for"*. Either delete the clause or replace it with the specific consequence ("so we lost two weeks of orders").

### Invented compound labels and quotable lines

Coined two-word concepts and slogan-like closers (*the trust gap*, *the intelligence layer*, *a quiet revolution*). Keep a label only if the author would use it in conversation and it will be used again.

### Performed candour

*honestly*, *to be frank*, *let's be real*, *here's the truth*. A real admission carries a detail; a stock one only announces itself.

### Promotional register in neutral text

Scenic, glowing or "premium" description (*breathtaking*, *vibrant*, *world-class*, *state-of-the-art*) where the text should simply inform. Wikipedia's editors flag this as typical of model prose.

### Handle with care

Do not strip these when they are doing real work: technical terms (*robust* in a spec, *scalable* in an architecture doc), quoted material, product names, legal language, and words a non-native writer uses correctly and formally. Formality is not a defect.
